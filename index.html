A house suspended between shelter, bridge, and frame.

One drawing becomes a field of structural questions. The triangle is not decoration here. It carries weight, directs movement, and opens space.

02 / Form

a span.

The widest line arrives first. Beneath it, the house narrows, gathers tension, and finds the ground.

03 / Tension

Three edges hold
a room in tension.

The inverted triangle feels suspended and grounded at once. Its weight is visible, but its center remains open.

04 / Movement

Movement cuts
through the frame.

The stair does more than connect levels. It becomes another structural stroke, carrying the eye beyond the house.

05 / Shelter

Structure is allowed
to speak.

Nothing is hidden. Roof, support, opening, and path become the atmosphere of the house.

06 / Direction reset

One structure.
Four consequences.

The elevations are no longer designed as façades.

The original east sketch now generates a continuous exoskeleton: its roof and frame turn north to make the entrance, widen south to span the family terrace, and compress west to protect the service edge.

01

East remains invariant

The inverted V, crossing diagonal, hangers and occupied upper floor are preserved and proportionally refined.

02

Structure turns corners

North, south and west are produced by the same perimeter frame rather than receiving decorative diagonal beams.

03

Rooms occupy the field

Glass and solid cores sit behind the load-bearing system. One controlled living void connects stair, bridge and roof aperture.

04

Engineering stays visible

Megaframe legs, steel roof chords, slab edges and slender hangers establish a legible conceptual load path.

06 / Plan

a house.

Four bedrooms. Two occupied floors. One precise living void.

The original right-side drawing now organizes a complete home: a protected northern arrival, a hybrid design studio, family rooms facing the southern garden, and the feature stair held behind the eastern frame.
