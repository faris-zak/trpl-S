A house suspended between shelter, bridge, and frame.

One drawing becomes a field of structural questions. The triangle is not decoration here. It carries weight, directs movement, and opens space.

02 / Form

A roof becomes
a span.

The widest line arrives first. Beneath it, the house narrows, gathers tension, and finds the ground.

03 / Tension

Three edges hold
a room in tension.

The inverted triangle feels suspended and grounded at once. Its weight is visible, but its center remains open.

04 / Movement

Movement cuts
through the frame.

The stair does more than connect levels. It becomes another structural stroke, carrying the eye beyond the house.

05 / Shelter

Structure is allowed
to speak.

Nothing is hidden. Roof, support, opening, and path become the atmosphere of the house.

06 / Plan

The section becomes
a house.

Four bedrooms. Two occupied floors. One precise living void.

The original right-side drawing now organizes a complete home: a protected northern arrival, a hybrid design studio, family rooms facing the southern garden, and the feature stair held behind the eastern frame.

07 / Elevations

One identity.
Four responses.

The house changes with privacy, sun and use.

The east remains iconic. The north becomes a composed arrival, the south opens to the pool and the west protects service rooms from heat and view.
