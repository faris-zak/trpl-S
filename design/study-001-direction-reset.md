# Study 001 - Architectural Direction Reset

## Position

Study 001 is not a villa with diagonal decoration. It is a house held inside a continuous structural field. The east sketch is the invariant: a floating roof, an occupied upper level, an inverted megaframe, slender roof supports, and a crossing diagonal that makes load and movement visible.

Every later decision must answer one question: does it strengthen the identity of trpl-S, or make the house ordinary?

## One-building strategy

The house is organized by a continuous perimeter exoskeleton rather than four facade compositions.

- A folded floating roof establishes one silhouette around the full building.
- Four corner anchors receive the roof loads and free the family spaces from dense internal columns.
- The east megaframe preserves the original inverted V and crossing diagonal.
- The east frame turns the northeast corner to form the protected arrival structure.
- The south anchors widen to frame the terrace, double-height living room and pool view.
- The west frame compresses into a calmer, more solid service edge without changing its structural grammar.
- Glass and solid rooms sit behind the exoskeleton; structural members are never pasted onto their surfaces.

## Massing

The building is an 18 x 13.5 m occupied volume beneath a 21.5 x 16.5 m roof field. Two offset floors sit behind the frame:

- The ground floor is open toward the south and east, with solid service and studio cores toward the west.
- The upper floor is fully occupied. A limited void is cut only above the family living room.
- The stair rises inside the east frame and lands on a bridge through the double-height space.
- A triangular roof aperture brings daylight onto the bridge and exposes the roof structure from inside.

## Structural concept

The concept uses three coordinated systems:

1. Pale concrete or composite megaframe legs carry the long roof edges to four perimeter anchors.
2. Graphite steel edge beams and roof trusses span between the megaframes.
3. Slender steel hangers stabilize the occupied upper-floor edge and keep the roof visually separate from the glass volumes.

Member depths, connections, lateral stability, deflection, thermal bridging and foundations remain subject to architect and structural-engineer development. The model expresses a credible load path but is not engineering documentation.

## Spatial consequences

- North: the frame opens around the family entrance and makes arrival legible from the road.
- East: stair, bridge, upper rooms and living void are seen through the original structural composition.
- South: the frame spans a continuous terrace and panoramic family glazing instead of reducing the elevation to a glass rectangle.
- West: service cores occupy the opaque bays while the roof edge and corner anchors preserve the shared silhouette.
- Interior: structure, bridge, stair and roof aperture remain visible from the main living spaces.

## Workflow gate

This massing and structural system must be reviewed before detailed room planning, facade detailing or photorealistic rendering. Previous elevation-led drawings are retained only as superseded studies and are removed from the active website narrative.
