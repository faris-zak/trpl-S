# trpl-S Logo Usage

Version 1.1 / selected direction: **Aperture**

## Idea

Three structural forms turn around a central triangular opening. The symbol is abstract and architectural; it does not hide initials or depict a literal house. The custom `trpl-S` wordmark is path-based and must not be replaced by typed text.

## Approved colors

- Warm graphite: `#292725` / RGB `41 39 37`
- White: `#FFFFFF` / RGB `255 255 255`
- One-ink print: `100% K`

Do not introduce accent colors, gradients, metallic effects, or texture inside the production mark.

## Configurations

- Use the horizontal lockup by default.
- Use the stacked lockup where width is constrained.
- Use the standalone symbol for favicons, established-brand contexts, and compact drawing stamps.
- Use graphite artwork on light fields and white artwork on graphite or dense drawing areas.

## Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space equal to one symbol frame thickness, called `X`, around every side.

- Symbol: `16px` digital / `10mm` print
- Horizontal lockup: `120px` digital / `32mm` print
- Drawing stamp: `42mm` recommended

Use `trpl-s-symbol-small.svg` for 16px and 32px contexts; its openings are optically reinforced.

## Drawing placement

Place the graphite stamp over pale paper or quiet drawing areas. Use the white stamp over dense graphite. Move the stamp to a quieter area when local contrast is insufficient; do not add shadows, glows, outlines, or enclosing badges.

## Do not

- Stretch, rotate, skew, outline, or rearrange the symbol.
- Recolor the mark or add effects.
- Typeset `trpl-S` in a substitute font.
- Place the mark over busy detail without adequate contrast.
