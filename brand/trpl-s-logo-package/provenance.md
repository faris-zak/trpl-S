# Provenance

- Brand text: `trpl-S`, preserved exactly.
- Creative source: the owner's pencil architectural drawing supplied in this thread.
- Source use: structural inspiration only; the drawing was not traced into the mark.
- Exploration routes: Truss, Span, Aperture, and Continuous Frame.
- Selected route: Aperture, chosen by the owner for its compact triangular knot, central opening, and energetic silhouette.
- Production method: deterministic SVG geometry and path-built lettering; PNG and PDF exports derive from the same masters.
- Palette: warm graphite `#292725`, white `#FFFFFF`, and black for one-ink printing only.
- Production logo contains no raster images, font references, gradients, filters, or texture.
